## Our Standards

This is an academic repository. All participants in Issues, Pull Requests, and
Discussions are expected to maintain the standards appropriate to scholarly
discourse. Specifically:

**Expected behavior:**
- Engage with ideas and arguments, not with personal attacks
- Acknowledge prior work and give credit where it is due
- Disagree respectfully and substantively
- Welcome diverse perspectives, including critique of the frameworks in this dissertation
- Disclose conflicts of interest relevant to your commentary

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Personal attacks, harassment, or intimidation of any kind
- Misrepresentation of the dissertation's claims or the author's positions
- Spam, promotional content, or off-topic submissions
- Use of this repository's Issues system to conduct or coordinate activities
  that violate the ethical use statement in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md)

---

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer reserves the right to close Issues, reject Pull
Requests, and remove participants who violate these standards, at their sole
discretion.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions within this GitHub repository,
including Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and comments on any of the above.
