# Support This Research — Donations

Thank you for your interest in supporting ongoing research in information
security theory, privacy engineering, and post-quantum cryptography.

This dissertation represents years of sustained independent scholarly inquiry.
If this work has been useful to you — in academic research, security engineering,
policy analysis, or simply as a rigorous reference — your support is deeply
appreciated and helps make future research possible.

---

## How Donations Are Used

100% of donations received through this repository are allocated exclusively to:

- **Research continuation** — Funding further theoretical work in the areas
  of anonymous network security, post-quantum cryptographic transitions, and
  formal threat modeling.
- **Open-access dissemination** — Covering costs associated with making future
  publications freely available to the academic community.
- **Simulation infrastructure** — Computational resources for ongoing empirical
  studies extending the Dark Topology Conjecture and related results.
- **Conference & peer review** — Submission fees and travel for presenting
  this research at academic security conferences (IEEE S&P, ACM CCS, USENIX
  Security, and related venues).

No portion of donations is used for personal expenses unrelated to research.

## Recognition

Donors who wish to be acknowledged may opt in to recognition in future
publications derived from this research. Please include your preferred name or
organization name in the donation notes, along with `[acknowledge: yes]`.

Donors may also choose to remain anonymous — no personal information is shared
or published without explicit consent.

## A Note of Genuine Gratitude

Academic research in foundational security theory does not carry the commercial
appeal of applied tooling or consumer products. It is sustained by the conviction
that rigorous theoretical frameworks are what eventually make better tools
possible.

Every contribution — however small — signals that this kind of work matters to
the community it serves. Thank you.
